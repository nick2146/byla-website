Why partner with BYLA?

A meaningful way to invest in practical learning.

Give students a chance to think about the kind of decisions businesses face every day.

Case partners provide a realistic business prompt and may contribute one or two representatives to the final judging panel. Students get a learning experience grounded in a real challenge, while partners get to hear fresh perspectives from emerging problem-solvers.

  • Introduce students to real business questions
  • Support accessible, hands-on business education
  • Connect with motivated students across schools
  • Optional sponsorship opportunities for event support
