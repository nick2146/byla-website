How it works

From curiosity to informed decisions.

Build familiarity with markets through a simple, educational paper-trading experience.

01 / EXPLORE

Research companies.

Learn what companies do, how they earn revenue, and why news and economic conditions can affect share prices.

02 / SIMULATE

Build a virtual portfolio.

Use simulated funds to practise buying and selling in a learning environment. No real money is deposited or invested.

03 / REFLECT

Review your decisions.

Consider diversification, volatility, risk, and the difference between short-term price changes and long-term business performance.

What students can learn

Practical concepts. Clear explanations.

The goal isn't to predict every market move—it's to learn how to ask better questions and understand financial trade-offs.

FINANCE

Stocks and ownership

What a share represents, why prices change, and how a public company is valued.

RISK

Diversification

Why holding different assets can affect portfolio risk, and why losses are possible.

RESEARCH

News and results

How earnings, competition, product launches, and economic news can influence expectations.
