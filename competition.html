Choose your lens

Three industry tracks. One clear recommendation.

Teams will work within one of three industry areas, using research and structured reasoning to propose an actionable solution.

TRACK A

Retail & E-Commerce

Customer experience, growth, pricing, merchandising, and digital sales.

TRACK B

Food & Hospitality

Service design, operations, customer loyalty, and local growth.

TRACK C

Technology & Services

Digital strategy, product-market fit, and service innovation.

Competition day

From case release to final pitch.

The scheduled event runs from noon to 3:00 PM on November 21, 2026. Review the official rulebook for the full requirements and any updates.

12:00 PM

Case release

Receive the case materials and multi-client briefing.

12:15 PM

Strategy sprint

75 minutes to research, plan, and build the five-slide deck.

1:30 PM

Submission deadline

Upload the deck by the hard deadline.

1:30 PM

Track semi-finals

2:00 PM

Awards & networking

Scoring rubric

100 points across five lenses.

The rubric rewards clear analysis, feasible recommendations, thoughtful risk assessment, and persuasive communication.

Awards

Recognition for the work behind the idea.

The total placement grant pool is $500: $300 for 1st place and $200 for 2nd place. Each placement grant is split equally across that winning team, not awarded per person. There is no 3rd-place cash grant.

1st place$300

Placement grant split equally across the winning team, plus a certificate.

2nd place$200

Placement grant split equally across the winning team, plus a certificate.

Special awards may include Best Financial Model, Best Visual System Design, and Most Innovative Go-to-Market Strategy. See the rulebook for award conditions, payout milestones, submission standards, and conduct expectations.
