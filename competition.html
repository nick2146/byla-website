BYLA Case Competition

BYLA Case Competition.
Many ways to lead.

A live business challenge for teams of 2–4 students in Grades 9–12. Analyze the problem, develop a recommendation, and present your thinking in a concise five-slide deck.

Choose your lens

Three industry tracks. One clear recommendation.

Teams will work within one of three industry areas, using research and structured reasoning to propose an actionable solution.

TRACK A

Retail & E-Commerce

Customer experience, growth, pricing, merchandising, and digital sales.

TRACK B

Food & Hospitality

Service design, operations, customer loyalty, and local growth.

TRACK C

Technology & Services

Digital strategy, product-market fit, and service innovation.

Competition day

From case release to final pitch.

The competition date and event schedule will be announced once confirmed. Team registration is open. review the competition details for requirements and updates.

12:00 PM

Case release

Receive the case materials and multi-client briefing.

12:15 PM

Strategy sprint

75 minutes to research, plan, and build the five-slide deck.

Submission deadline

Upload the deck by the announced deadline.

1:30 PM

Track semi-finals

Partner reviewers select one Track Champion per track.

2:00 PM

Grand finals

Track Champions pitch for four minutes, followed by three minutes of judge Q&A.

2:45 PM

Awards & networking

Recognize teams and connect with participating business partners.

Team size: 2–4 studentsDeck: 5 slidesPitch: 4 minutesQ&A: 3 minutes
Scoring rubric

100 points across five lenses.

The rubric rewards clear analysis, feasible recommendations, thoughtful risk assessment, and persuasive communication.

Problem Identification & Analysis15 pts
Strategic Innovation & Execution25 pts
Financial Viability20 pts
Risk Assessment15 pts
Presentation Delivery & Q&A25 pts
Total possible score100 pts
Awards

Recognition for the work behind the idea.

Teams will be recognized for strong analysis, creative thinking, clear communication, and practical recommendations.

01 / STRATEGY

Best Overall Recommendation

For a clear, well-supported response to the business challenge.

02 / ANALYSIS

Outstanding Business Analysis

For thoughtful use of evidence, assumptions, and business reasoning.

03 / INNOVATION

Most Innovative Approach

For an original, practical idea backed by a strong execution plan.
