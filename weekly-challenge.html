LAUNCH EDITION · LEARNING THEMES

This launch edition introduces themes to watch rather than claiming to be a live news feed. Follow the linked newsrooms for the latest reporting; new BYLA editions can be manually updated each week.

01 / ECONOMY$

Interest rates and the cost of doing business

When borrowing becomes more or less expensive, businesses may reconsider expansion, hiring, inventory, and prices.

Why this matters

Financial decisions depend on cash flow, the cost of capital, and how confident customers feel about spending.

Follow Bank of Canada updates ↗
02 / ENTREPRENEURSHIP↗

How small businesses find their edge

Smaller companies often compete by serving a specific audience better, offering a distinct experience, or solving a problem larger rivals overlook.

What students should learn

A strong business idea starts with a clear customer problem—not simply a product someone thinks is cool.

Explore CBC Business coverage ↗
03 / TECHNOLOGY⌘

AI, productivity, and customer trust

New tools can help teams work faster, but businesses still need to evaluate quality, privacy, cost, and customer expectations.

Why this matters

Adopting technology is a strategy decision: the benefit must outweigh the risks and implementation costs.

CHALLENGE OF THE WEEK
01 / MARKETING + FINANCE

A local café needs to grow—while staying sustainable.

A neighbourhood café has seen fewer afternoon customers. The owner wants to attract more visitors without cutting prices across the entire menu.

ConstraintLimited resources
GoalMore afternoon visits
ConstraintProtect profit margin

Your task

Choose the approach you would test first. Think about the target customer, expected cost, how you would measure success, and what could go wrong.
