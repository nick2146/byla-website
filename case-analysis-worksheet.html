Business Case Analysis Worksheet

Use this worksheet to organize your thinking before preparing a recommendation or presentation.

Back to Learning Hub

1. What is the core problem?

What decision does the business need to make? What is causing the problem?

2. What evidence matters most?

List key facts, customer insights, financial data, constraints, and assumptions.

3. What options could the business choose?

Compare alternatives, including benefits, costs, risks, and trade-offs.

4. What do you recommend?

State one clear recommendation and explain why it is the strongest option.

5. How will the plan be implemented?

List first steps, owners, timeline, approximate budget, and success measures.

6. What could go wrong?

Identify major risks and how you would reduce or respond to them.
