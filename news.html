Read like a business leader

Don’t stop at the headline.

When you read a story, ask:

  • What actually happened, and what evidence supports it?
  • Who is affected: customers, workers, businesses, or governments?
  • What could happen next, and what remains uncertain?
  • Which other credible source confirms the key facts?
