Our purpose

Business education should feel real, not distant.

We want students to see business as something they can explore, question, and contribute to today.

BYLA connects Grades 9–12 students with case-based challenges across strategy, market research, financial reasoning, digital strategy, risk, entrepreneurship, and presentation. Students are encouraged to turn research into practical recommendations and learn from the feedback they receive.

We aim to support youth confidence, strengthen local business connections, and expand access to meaningful business education through competitions and future learning opportunities.

What guides us

A few principles shape every experience.

The point is not to arrive knowing everything. It is to ask better questions, make reasoned choices, and improve through practice.

01 / ACCESS

Open doors.

No DECA chapter, school club, or faculty advisor is required to participate. Cross-school teams are welcome.

02 / OWNERSHIP

Student ideas first.

Teams take responsibility for their research, recommendations, slide decks, and presentations.

03 / INTEGRITY

Learn with purpose.

Professional conduct, fair judging, transparent expectations, and respectful collaboration matter.

Who can participate?

Curious students are welcome.

BYLA's competition is designed for students in Grades 9–12. Teams typically include 2–4 students, and cross-school or cross-grade collaboration is encouraged where competition rules allow.

You do not need to have started a business or taken a business course. You need curiosity, a willingness to work with others, and an interest in learning how to solve a problem.
