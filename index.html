More than learning business terms.

We want students to understand how business works in the real world—and feel ready to participate in it.

  • ✓Business learning: build a foundation in marketing, finance, strategy, and entrepreneurship.
  • ✓Case challenges: practise analyzing problems and presenting thoughtful solutions.

  • ✓Community: learn with other students who are curious about business.

Think. Analyze. Present.

Meet the challenge of a business case.

The BYLA Case Competition is designed to give student teams an opportunity to apply business thinking to a realistic challenge and communicate their recommendations.

Understand the problem

Review the case, identify the central challenge, and determine what matters most.

Build your strategy

Collaborate with your teammates to develop a clear, evidence-informed recommendation.

Present your solution

Organize your thinking and communicate your proposed strategy with confidence.
