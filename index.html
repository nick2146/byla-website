Meet BYLA

Make room for the next generation of business thinkers.

BYLA is a student-led, non-profit initiative connecting young people with practical business learning and real challenges from the world around them.

Teams explore strategy, research, financial reasoning, innovation, and communication. The goal is bigger than a competition: build confidence, practice collaboration, and help students turn what they learn into ideas they can defend.

  • Open enrollment, no school club required
  • Cross-school teams and collaborative learning
  • Business cases, judging, feedback, and recognition

The BYLA experience

A challenge that builds more than a slide deck.

Practice the skills that matter when a good idea has to become a clear, realistic recommendation.

01 / STRATEGY

Think critically.

Understand the business problem, evaluate the evidence, and choose a direction with a clear rationale.

02 / COLLABORATION

Build together.

Share roles, test assumptions, manage time, and bring different perspectives into one focused solution.

03 / COMMUNICATION

Present with purpose.

Explain your recommendation, support it with reasoning, and respond thoughtfully to judge questions.
