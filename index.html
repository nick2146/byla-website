About BYLA

Not just another competition. A launchpad.

BYLA connects Grades 9–12 students to real-world challenges in strategy, market research, financial modeling, digital strategy, risk, entrepreneurship, consulting and executive presentation. It empowers youth, supports local business and expands access to business education.

No school club, DECA chapter or faculty advisor is required — open enrollment with cross-school teams means eligible students can take part. Registration is a flat $5 per person.

Competition Format

One day. One live sprint. One clear timeline.

Everything happens on November 20, 2026 — case release, the 75-minute strategy sprint, semi-finals and grand finals all run the same day from 12:00–3:00 PM. Teams pick one of three industry tracks and build a 5-slide deck.

TRACK A

Retail & E-Commerce

Customer experience, growth, pricing and digital sales.

TRACK B

Food & Hospitality

Service design, operations, customer loyalty and local growth.

TRACK C

Tech & Services

Digital strategy, product-market fit and service innovation.

2:00 PM

Track Champions pitch live — 4 minutes plus 3 minutes of judge Q&A.

2:45 PM

Awards & Networking

Placement grants, special awards and networking with partner businesses.

Scoring Rubric

100 points. Five lenses.

Scored out of 100 across five pillars. Strong = evidence-backed and defensible; developing = plausible but incomplete; weak = unsupported or materially flawed.

Problem Identification & Analysis15 pts
Strategic Innovation & Execution25 pts
Financial Viability20 pts
Risk Assessment15 pts
Presentation Delivery & Q&A25 pts
Total possible score100 pts
WEIGHT 25%

Strategic Innovation & Execution

Key focus: creativity, modern tactics and solution fit. Top-band score: strategic originality and execution that is evidence-backed and defensible.
