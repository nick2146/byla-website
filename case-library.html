Practice library

Cases built for learning.

Use these fictional practice briefs to learn the structure of business analysis. They are not real client cases or official competition prompts.

Marketing · Beginner

The quiet café

A neighbourhood café has strong weekend traffic but empty weekday afternoons. The owner has a limited promotional budget and wants repeat visits without discounting every item.

Your challenge: identify the target customer, propose two options and choose measurable success metrics.

Operations · Intermediate

The late deliveries

A small online store receives good reviews for its products, but delivery delays are causing refunds. The team cannot afford to hire many new employees.

Your challenge: find likely root causes, compare process changes and define a realistic implementation plan.

Analyze this case ↗
Finance · Intermediate

The growing maker

A student-run custom product business is getting more orders but has less cash available. Material costs and shipping fees are rising.

Your challenge: separate profit from cash flow and recommend a pricing or operating decision.

Analyze this case ↗
Strategy · Intermediate

The app that isn't growing

A small study-planning app has positive feedback from a few users but struggles to attract new customers. The founders have limited time and marketing funds.

Your challenge: identify a specific target segment and design a low-cost experiment to test demand.
