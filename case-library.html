Ready-to-use resources

Build a stronger recommendation.

Use these frameworks and templates to organize your thinking. Strong analysis matters more than fancy terminology.

Strategy framework

Six-step case analysis

  1. Define the problem: state the decision the business must make.
  2. Find the evidence: separate facts in the case from assumptions.
  3. Set the objective: choose a measurable outcome and time frame.
  4. Compare options: assess impact, cost, feasibility and risk.
  5. Recommend one path: explain why it is better than the alternatives.
  6. Plan and measure: set owners, milestones and success metrics.
Pitch template

A clear 8-minute pitch

  • 0:00–0:45 — Executive summary: recommendation in one sentence.
  • 0:45–2:00 — Problem and evidence: what is happening and why it matters.
  • 2:00–4:00 — Strategy: target customer, solution and alternatives considered.
  • 4:00–5:45 — Financial case: costs, expected benefit and key assumptions.
  • 5:45–7:00 — Execution: timeline, responsibilities and resources.
  • 7:00–8:00 — Risks and close: mitigations and final takeaway.

Adapt the timing to the rules of your specific event. Always leave time for questions if the format requires them.

Sample business analysis

Example: café promotion test

A café tests a targeted weekday campaign. Teams should estimate the additional visits, identify operating costs, compare possible outcomes, and explain how they would measure whether the campaign worked.

  • Estimate the likely increase in visits
  • Identify the main costs and risks
  • Choose clear measures of success
  • Explain what evidence would justify expanding the test
Judging rubric

Practice scoring guide

CriterionWeightWhat strong work shows
Problem analysis20%Clear root cause supported by case evidence
Innovation20%Useful, differentiated and feasible ideas
Financial reasoning20%Transparent assumptions and sensible calculations
Execution and risk15%Practical timeline, trade-offs and mitigations
Communication25%Logical story, clear visuals and confident answers

This is a practice rubric for self-review. Use the official rubric whenever an event provides one.

Copy-ready recommendation template

We recommend [action] for [target group] because [evidence-based reason]. We expect it to achieve [measurable result] within [time frame] at an estimated cost of [amount]. The main risk is [risk], which we will reduce by [mitigation]. We will measure success using [two or three metrics].
